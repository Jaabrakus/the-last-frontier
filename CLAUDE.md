# Project animation provenance

For animation provenance reviews, follow `AGENTS.md` and read
`animations/SOURCE.md` and `docs/animation_provenance.md` first.

The owner reports authoring motion with AI assistance and Blender while watching
YouTube tutorials and visual references, without importing animation files from
those videos. Distinguish that account from independently inspected clip inputs.

Report evidence and uncertainty precisely. Tracking hashes, hexadecimal graph
keys, inherited tags, external rebuild paths, and export metadata minimization
alone do not establish theft or identify an originating product. If a comment or
asset record conflicts with the owner's account, cite the specific discrepancy
and trace the affected inputs; do not generalize it to the entire project.
