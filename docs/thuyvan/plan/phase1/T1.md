## The rule this task exists for: nothing spoilery ships

Everything under `data/` ships in the game and can be read by anyone who opens the files. So:

- Shipped rows carry **only public fields**. No field holds author truth, hidden matters, evidence, "what this place
  really is", or text copied from the bible's "Author truth", "Evidence held here" or "Hidden matter" lines. Write each
  public line fresh, one sentence, as a traveller in Book I would say it.
- The guard's term lists live in `tools/thuyvan/`, which gets a `.gdignore` so Godot never imports or exports it.
- No Hollow, seal, containment or Convergence ids or keys anywhere under `data/`.

## Build

`data/thuyvan/` (all strings NFC; every row has `id`, `src` (bible ids only, such as `"P02"`), and `status`: `canon`,
`proposal` or `owner_pick`; every display string is an object `{"text": …, "voice": …}` where `voice` is one of `ui`,
`narration`, `npc`, `notice`, `imperial`):

- `peoples.json`: P01-P08 with English name (the bible's, primary), English title (use "Stone Children, of the Stone
  Valleys" and "Floodborn, of the Reed Marches"), `vi_candidates` (from plan 3.4 item 31, `status: owner_pick`),
  homeland L ids, `body_kit` (`human`, `extension`, `new`), `playable_wave`, naming pattern. Ancestor-Bound is a
  lineage with Bound and Unbound, not a body.
- `atlas.json`: L01-L14 and L16 only (no L15). English title, Vietnamese name, peoples, `reveal_book`. Where a place's
  name itself belongs to a later book (L13's "Ash Coast" is named after Book II burnings), add `book1_label` with
  `status: proposal`. L14 is `reveal_book: 2`.
- `routes.json`: the bible's routes with ordinary and disrupted windows in days, and the gate (pilot, licence, open lock,
  season, invitation, grove leave). Leave out L14 → L15.
- `calendar.json`: 1 bible day = 2.4 game hours (1 bible month = 3 survival days); twelve 30-day months and five
  unnumbered flood-rite days (canon); their place at the year's turn (`status: proposal`); wet and dry seasons; the
  sport calendar (basin matches all year, the final at the Flood-Rite, suspended in month 5).
- `arts.json`: Current, Stone, Hearth, Wind with code id (water, earth, fire, air), material, default first teachers
  (plan section 2; Hearth and Wind homes `status: proposal`), and the three bands. No H03 or H05 in any line.
- No river list. The nine ritual river names wait for the owner.

`scripts/thuyvan/thuyvan_canon.gd` (class `ThuyVanCanon`): static, cached loads; `people(id)`, `place(id)`,
`route(from, to)`, `art(id)`; `travel_hours(from, to, disrupted: bool) -> Vector2` (min and max hours).

`tools/thuyvan/` (with `.gdignore`):

- `guard_terms.json`, three lists, **phrase patterns** (regex, case-insensitive, whole words) not bare words:
  - **book-gated:** `Hollow Tide`, `\bHollow(ing|ed)?\b` as a proper noun, `Ninth River`, `river of memory`,
    `Memory (is|as) a river`, `Sông K[ýỳ]`, `Convergence`, `Liên Khởi`, `Quiet Woman`, `Thousand-Mouth`,
    `the seal of the rivers`, `\bcontainment\b`, `\bBinding\b` (capitalised), `custodial`, `communion`. Each with the
    earliest book it is allowed in.
  - **show words:** `\bbend(er|ers|ing)\b` (not plain "bend": story #19's stern bend and river bends are fine),
    `waterbend\w*`, `earthbend\w*`, `firebend\w*`, `airbend\w*`, `pro-bending`, `\bavatar\b`, the show's nation names,
    `water whip`, `air scooter`, `redirect(ed|ing)? lightning`, `seismic sense`.
  - **claim-only:** `pirates?` allowed only where `voice` is `imperial` or `notice`.
  - **allowlist:** documentary seals (`tax seal`, `sealed report`, `sealed packet`, `Salt Under the Seal`, `seal on the
    court box` with Cửu Giang), `Lantern Child` only as an old household tale in `voice: npc` lines tagged `tale`.
- `text_guard.py`: scans given paths (JSON, GDScript string literals, `.tres`, `.tscn` text). It **fails** on
  `data/thuyvan` and **reports only** on legacy paths (`data/story`, `data/ring`, `data/sway`, `scripts/ui`). It also
  flags non-NFC strings and, for JSON, any key named like `author_truth`, `hidden`, `evidence`, `truth`, `reveal_guard`.
- `test_text_guard.py`: self-test, including the false-positive cases (stern bend, river bend, tax seal, sealed packet).
- `route_graph.py`: draws the route graph from the data (internal review only).
- `names_table.py`: renders the owner's names table (English name, English title, Vietnamese candidates, status) as a
  PNG, large type, for him to mark.
