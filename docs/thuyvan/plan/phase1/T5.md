## Build

**1. A region loader (small edits to shared files; defaults unchanged).**

- New `scripts/world/world_region.gd` (class `WorldRegion`): a static region id (empty = today), and the paths that
  depend on it: squares file, terrain data dir, layers dir.
- `scripts/world/world_squares.gd` (`PATH`), `scripts/world/terrain/terrain.gd` (`DATA_DIR`) and
  `scripts/world/story_layer.gd` (`DIR`) read their path from `WorldRegion`, falling back to today's constants.
- `data/world/maps/r1_grey/squares.json`: today's `data/world/squares.json` content (same grid, types and river block,
  so today's baked terrain and river bed still match) plus a `reaches` list and a `region` block (name, latitude,
  season). The terrain data dir stays today's.
- `data/world/maps/r1_grey/layers/`: S0 and S1 layer files.

**2. The still reach (small edits to shared files; off unless a reach is set).**

- `WorldRiver`: per-reach flow scale read from the river block's `reaches` (`z_from`, `z_to`, `ease_m`,
  `scale_by_state`); `flow_at` multiplies by it, so `WaterSurface.current_at` returns zero inside a still reach and
  boats drift to a stop. `still_amount(z) -> float` for the others.
- `river_water.gd` and `shaders/water/river.gdshader`: a still-band uniform: flow and ripple speed eased to 0 in the
  band, clarity raised so the white stones show.
- `scripts/wildlife/fish/fish_life.gd`: fish inside the band hang suspended (no swim, no flee).
- The river's flow sound (find its owner; `scripts/audio/ambience_bed.gd` is the first place to look) mutes inside
  the band, so near-silence is the effect.
- Held mist: static fog cards placed by the level over the band, which do not drift.
- A cordon in S1: a few NPC watchers and a rope line at the band's lower end that turn the player back (plan section 2:
  what touching the water does stays unanswered).

**3. The preview level.**

- `scripts/thuyvan/level/thuyvan_level.gd` and `scenes/thuyvan_preview.tscn`: a trimmed copy of `frontier_level.gd`
  that sets `WorldRegion` to `r1_grey` before anything loads; keeps the environment rig, terrain, river, vegetation,
  survival, forage, fish, birds, fireflies and footsteps; leaves out the fort and structures, the tandem and puppy
  features, the story slice, the wonder sky, the wildlife spawner (its animals are North American), SaveGame (no saving in the greybox, so the frontier save
  is never touched) and weapon audio.
- WorldClock set from the level: latitude about 21° N, an early-rains day of year, morning start (`latitude_deg` is an
  export; no edit to `world_clock.gd` should be needed).
- **S0 ↔ S1** on a raw debug key (F9) in the level script, switching the reach scale and the layer in place.
- **No guns:** a `no_firearms` flag on `FrontierPlayer` that only skips building the guns, the gun IK and the musket
  loadout. Every world screen (H, Tab, K), the equip wheel, building and the household stay. Do **not** reuse the
  `arena` flag; it turns those screens off.
- **A Mountain Folk preset** applied through the creator's existing preset machinery (`scripts/customizer/peoples.gd`)
  with today's garments closest to the Common Folk sheet. No new garments, no new textures.
- `tools/thuyvan/play_thuyvan.command`: a double-click launcher that opens Godot (not headless) on the lane with
  `scenes/thuyvan_preview.tscn`.

**4. Shots for the owner** (`tools/thuyvan_shots/`, run under the lock):

- `mood_frames.gd`: one camera at the still reach, rendered three ways: today's look; a painterly grade (fog, tonemap,
  colour adjustments on the WorldEnvironment only); and the loop's toon preset **only if** `tb/LOOK-A` has gated into
  spellcraft and been merged into `thuyvan` (otherwise put the loop's own LOOK-A review image beside the two).
- `still_vs_running.gd`: the same camera, S0 vs S1, at dawn, noon and in rain.
