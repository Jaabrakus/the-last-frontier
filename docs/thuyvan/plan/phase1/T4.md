## Build

`tools/environment/terrain/regions/`:

- `common.py`: copies of the generic helpers (noise, fbm, smoothstep, polyline and segment distance), credited to
  `bake_terrain.py` in a comment.
- `recipes_thuyvan.py`, in this priority (the slice first):
  1. `gorge` (C1), `terraced_slope` with flat paddy steps (B1), `middle_reach` with a ferry bank (C2), `switchbacks`
     (B2), `rim` (A4, B4) — full recipes
  2. `high_pass`, `bridge_gorge`, `stone_height`, `foothill_paddies`, `river_flats_lock`, `river_exit_karst` — simple
     placeholders that meet the layout's levels and seams, flagged `placeholder` (M2 finishes them)

  Karst towers and sheer faces are meshes in Phase 3; here, mark only their footprints.
- `bake_region.py`: the same grid and seam rules as `bake_terrain.py` (833 × 833 at 1 m, HALF 416, MARGIN 64,
  TYPE_BLEND 100 m, the same output formats). It reads the cells from `data/world/maps/r1/squares.json` and carves
  channel beds and pounds:
  - until T3's first commit lands: through a thin adapter that reads `layout.json`'s channel nodes directly
  - before merging: through T3's `tools/environment/water/water_net.py`, and the adapter is deleted
- `relief_views.py`: shaded perspective renders of the height fields with flat water planes (matplotlib or PIL), for
  the owner.

`data/world/maps/r1/squares.json`: the twelve cells of the layout with their types and recipe parameters.

Output goes to `tools/environment/terrain/build_regions/r1/`, with `tools/environment/terrain/build_regions/.gitignore`
ignoring `*.bin` and every other bake output.

## Tests

`tools/environment/terrain/regions/test_regions.py` (you run it; re-run after switching to `water_net.py`):

- rebakes from the seed are bit-identical
- shared edges of neighbouring squares match within 1 cm
- beds sit under the water surface along every channel, by at least the layout's bed depth
- pounds and paddy steps are flat within 2 cm
- heights stay within each recipe's bounds
- the main channel's bed never rises downstream (it may drop sharply at the marked lock step)
