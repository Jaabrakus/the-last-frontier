# Takedown notice template

A template for asking a store, host or platform to remove a copy of Frontier, or content ripped from it. It follows
the notice-and-takedown requirements of the US DMCA (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)). Most platforms outside the US accept a
notice in the same form, and many have their own web forms: use the platform's form when it has one.

It is a draft: have a lawyer check it before first use. Only send a notice for material you own or are authorised to
act for. A knowingly false notice can make you liable. That includes third-party material in the game, such as the
extracted animation data and CC-BY models listed in CREDITS.md: those belong to their owners.

---

**To:** [PLATFORM'S DESIGNATED COPYRIGHT AGENT / ABUSE CONTACT]

**Subject:** Copyright infringement notice: [SHORT DESCRIPTION, e.g. "unauthorised copy of the game Frontier"]

1. **The copyrighted work.** Frontier, a video game by [STUDIO OR OWNER LEGAL NAME], including its [code / 3D models
   / textures / audio / artwork / text]. [Where the original can be seen: store page, website or trailer URL.]

2. **The infringing material and where it is.**
   - [URL 1]: [what it is, e.g. "a re-upload of the full game", "models extracted from the game"]
   - [URL 2]: [...]

3. **My contact details.** [Name, postal address, phone number, email]

4. I have a good faith belief that the use of the material described above is not authorised by the copyright
   owner, its agent, or the law.

5. The information in this notice is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, I am the owner, or authorised to act on
   behalf of the owner, of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

**Signature:** [Physical or electronic signature: typing your full legal name is usually accepted]

**Date:** [DATE]

---

Keep a copy of every notice you send and every reply, with dates.
