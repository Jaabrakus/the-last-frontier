# Frontier codebase audit: what the story can use now, and what it still needs

I only read files and ran light `grep`, `ls` and `git log` commands. I did not launch Godot or run any build. The report makes no new historical claims. Where it repeats a date or number from the repo's own drafts, that figure is marked "verify" in those drafts and has not been checked here.

## Bottom line

The sandbox is strong: survival, building, hunting, horses, boats, snow, climbing and the bow are all in place, and the Prologue's missions use them well. What the story is missing is **people**. There is no human NPC who can patrol, notice the player, fight, flee or be talked to in the world. There are no dialogue choices, no fire that burns buildings, no crime or witness system, and the game has only one map. Most later missions (the raid, the burning, the pack-train ambushes, the Ford chain) need at least the first two of these.

## Frame budget on the owner's M4 Mac mini

- **Default quality is medium**, described in the code as "a steady 60 fps on an Apple M4 Mac mini". The forest frame is 12-17 ms on medium against 26-35 ms on high (`world_settings.gd:10-12`, benchmark of 2026-09-28).
- The game is capped at 60 fps by default (commit e5d87c3).
- Animal animation was about 29 ms of a 35 ms frame. After animation level of detail, the steady level frame went from about 36 ms to about 26 ms (commit 91311ff; `wild_animal.gd:125`). I believe this was measured on high, because tool runs default to high, but the commit does not say.
- `frame_probe.gd:248` notes the level's frame swings between 35 and 115 ms, so it has hitches.
- **People are expensive:**
  - A full player animation tree costs about 2 ms a frame (`docs/multiplayer_readiness.md:56`).
  - Each torch light adds 0.55 ms, or 1.4 ms with its shadow (`torch_fire.gd:13`).
  - The backlog's NPC budget is 16 full NPCs, 40 later, and at most 12 with an animation tree (`data/survival/backlog/npcs.json`, architecture budget).
  - Spouse and worker bodies have no animation level of detail (nothing in `spouse.gd`).
- **What this means for raid scenes:** about 10-16 fighters on screen at most, using AnimationPlayer bodies rather than full player rigs, with level of detail like the animals'. The rest of a big force should be sound, fire glow, smoke and off-screen records. Fires should use one shared light with no shadows.

## Missing features, in priority order

1. **Human NPC fighter and brain** — size **L**. Patrol, perceive, raise an alert, fight with musket, bow or axe, take cover, flee, surrender, die and be looted. Make household NPCs hittable too, so "anyone can be attacked" holds. Add animation level of detail. Builds on FilmActor (aim, fire, hit, go down), HuntRun, HuntAim and WorkerGear, the Spouse body with NavWorld and WalkGrid, the animal senses, MeleeDuelAI, AxeMelee, `Hit.deliver`, wounds and ragdolls, and `WildAnimal._step_anim` for the level of detail. This unlocks the playable raid, ambushes, Vane's toughs, the ferry defence and the pack-train raids.
2. **Objective kinds and mission flow** — size **M**. `talk`, `kill` (people and named targets), `escort`, `defend` (waves or a timer), `survive_until`, `chase`, `deliver` and `steal`, `stay_unseen`, `follow`. Fail rules, a checkpoint saved at mission start, branching `next` and choices, `when` conditions, and effects as data (spawn a party, change world state, give or take items, change standing). Builds on StoryDirector, StoryObjectives, `SaveParts.save_story`, `SurvivalClock.in_window` and `SurvivalEconomy.add_standing`.
3. **Dialogue box with choices, contact markers and barks** — size **M**. Speaker, text, 2-4 choices with conditions and effects; trade opens as one branch. Builds on StoryHUD cards, the RecruitScreen and HouseholdScreen patterns, `lines.json`, `npcs.json` dialogue topics and SurvivalTrade.
4. **Bodies in the world for the slice cast** — size **M**. Placed by the existing schedules, with trade and lesson windows. This is the prerequisite for contacts. Builds on SurvivalNpcs, the Spouse and RecruitBody bodies, the peoples presets and garments, SurvivalTrade and SurvivalLearning.
5. **Fire and ruin** — size **M**. Built pieces and fort pieces catch fire, spread fire to neighbours, burn through stages and collapse to a ruin; a smoke column is visible from far off; the save keeps it. Cheap lighting. Builds on TorchFire, Campfire, TorchState fuel, `BuildWorld.remove_piece`, the snow shelter rebuild, `SaveParts.save_building` and NetSession `world_event`. Needed for the Prologue raid, Part 2, "The Hut in the Snow", "Fire at the Flatts" and "March Out".
6. **Captivity** — size **S-M**. Take gear into a store and return it, a bound or escorted state (no weapons, a captor to follow), a forced march, an escape choice. Builds on `BareStart.apply`, PackDrop, DeathRule, the loadout and NavMover following.
7. **Witnesses, hue and cry, and reputation tags** — size **M-L**, needs items 1 and 4. Builds on the standing tiers and penalties, per-NPC trust, the backlog's era alignments, the animal awareness model and `SnowPrints.prints_near` for pursuers tracking in snow.
8. **Map framework** — size **L**: framework **M**, plus **L** content per map. A map id selects its squares file, structures and places; year layers; reloading the level with the character, pack and household kept; a 2D travel map with days passing; a date jump between parts. Builds on WorldSquares, `bake_terrain.py`, FrontierLevel, SaveGame and SaveParts, the `scene=` argument and SurvivalCore's start options and `advance`.
9. **Cutscene staging inside the game** — size **M**. Camera paths, actors playing clips, letterbox, skip; must not pause in co-op. Builds on `trailer.gd` and `clips.gd` takes, FilmActor and the RtsMode camera.
10. **Mounted combat and NPC riders** — size **M-L**. Pistol or bow from the saddle, chases, pack trains. Builds on Horse, HorseMount and HorseRiderLink, `player.mount`, HuntRide, HorseCart and CarcassPack.
11. **AI boats and crews** — size **M**. Builds on `Boat.drive` and `crew_on`, and BoatRider.
12. **Bear, moose and beaver for the North Woods** — size **M** each. Builds on WildlifeSpecies and WildAnimal; WildHorse already runs other species on the horse's rig.
13. **Quest log, journal and map tab** — size **S**. Builds on the inventory screen's tabs, `SurvivalState.journal` and the director's places.
14. **The player learning from an NPC mentor** — size **S-M**. Bow, tracking, canoe, snowshoes. Builds on SurvivalLearning (taught and watching), the Teaching curriculum and `station_practice.gd`.
15. **Large interior kits** — size **M-L** each. Tavern, warehouse, courthouse; a longhouse or bark house only with the nation's office. Builds on FortHollow and the structure mesh kit.
16. **Story cards that do not pause in co-op, and shared squad missions** — size **S-M**. `story_hud.gd:160`.
17. **Player ageing from 1757 to 1775** — size **S-M**. Builds on the customizer blend shapes and the Growth stages.

## Where the records disagree

- The missions file's summary says a mission starts when its `when` holds, but StoryDirector never reads `when` (`story_director.gd:120-136`).
- `docs/multiplayer_readiness.md:3` still says "no networking in the game yet", while the same doc lists P1 to P6 as built.
- The command-mode header says a click on "a foe: attack it" (`rts_mode.gd`), but `rts_orders.gd:8` says that order comes later.
- `docs/main_story.md` lists snowshoes among the lessons as if they were a playable item; they exist only in the survival logic.
