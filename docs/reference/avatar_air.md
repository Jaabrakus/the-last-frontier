# Air: how it is gathered and shaped (reference)

Written 2026-10-17 by the element team's reference finder. **Word note:** reference doc, not player text; it uses
the show's words (airbending, air scooter, air sphere, the characters' names). Player strings never do

**Sources and honesty.** Technique names, users and episodes come from the Avatar Wiki's Airbending page and its

## 1. The art behind it

- Air is Baguazhang ("circle walking", eight trigram palm) with a hint of xingyiquan: "smooth coiling and uncoiling
  actions", dynamic footwork, open-hand techniques and throws, moving round the opponent and behind them. Staff work
  comes from northern gunshu: swinging, flowering, slicing. (Wiki: Connection; Sifu Kisu's team chose it for the
  air nomads' evasive, circular character.)
- The principle is evasion and flow: "airbenders ... build up a large amount of energy and momentum, which is released
  through powerful moves"; "air increases with larger sweeps and spins, using the momentum of their movement to
  produce larger gusts". Air is the opposite of earth: always moving, never rooted, "like a leaf in the wind"
  (airbending gates in "A Leaf in the Wind"). (Wiki: Air manipulation; Opposing bending art; Spirituality.)
- Air is everywhere, so there is no source problem; airbenders also augment moves with a staff, glider or fans, which
  focuses the current into blades. (Wiki: Strengths; Weapons.)
- Airbenders feel the air around them (hypersensitivity), so they sense blows before they land. (Wiki: Special
  techniques.)

## 2. How air is gathered

- **By sweeping circles.** The arms (or the staff) draw one or two wide circles in front of or around the body; the
  waist and hips turn through the circle, the feet step in an arc. A swirl of pale lines follows the hands; at the
  end of the sweep the lines contract inward to the palms or the staff tip. The wiki's "air ball": "a compressed ball
  of air by moving their hands together in a circular motion". Typical: 8-16 frames (0.3-0.7 s) for a basic blast,
  longer for a sphere or a tornado.
- **By running in a circle.** "By running in a circle and instantly building massive momentum, a master airbender can
  shoot a blast of highly compressed air shaped like the user's body" (concussive wake, Aang vs Zuko in "The
  Crossroads of Destiny"); "race in circles to create a cyclone designed to pull in arrows" (returning wind). The
  gather here is the whole body's path, with a ring of dust left on the ground.
- **By spinning the body.** A jump with a spin wraps the bender in a cocoon of wind (air cocoon); a stamped landing
  sends a shockwave (air bomb, shockwave); a kick in mid-air throws a tornado (Aang vs Combustion Man in "The Western
  Air Temple").
- **By twirling two fingers.** "Aircupressure": air wound and compressed into small bullets (Tagah; wiki). A tiny,
  fast, precise gather.
- **By compressing.** "Explosion of air: bringing air into a tight, compact form and then letting it loose";
  Avatar-level compression forms the sphere. A tight swirl shrinks to a bead, holds a beat, then bursts.
- **By jabbing or pulling.** The "thundering gust": "jab at the air around them to send it hurtling forward"; air
  pull: a beckoning hand brings objects or people towards the bender (Aang taking the fans in "The Warriors of
  Kyoshi").
- **From the breath.** "Breath of wind": a jet from the mouth that can blow an animal whistle or cool magma (Aang,
  Roku); needs extremely good breath control. Heat regulation by breathing.
- What the eye sees during the gather (memory): concentric pale arcs round the limbs, leaves and dust lifting and
  spiralling toward the hands, cloth and hair streaming opposite the sweep, and the bender's own body smeared along
  the circle.

## 3. The shapes of the classic moves

"Ours" is the game's move the shape maps to (section 7).

- **Air blast / palm strike / kick** (ours: Palm, Slice for the blade version). "A direct pulse or jet of compressed
  air shot from the hands, feet, or mouth ... generated more from the bender's own power rather than assisted by
  momentum; reaches further with greater accuracy." Drawn as a translucent cone or lozenge with a rim, stretched on
  the release frame (smear), leaving a ring or two of pale arcs; the target bends, dust blows (memory). Aang's kick
  shatters a catapulted rock ("Winter Solstice, Part 2"); two blasts at once block Combustion Man ("The Beach").
  Release 2 frames; travel 6-10; the cone thins to lines and fades in 4-8 frames.
- **Air punch / air kick** (ours: Palm). Small compressed puffs fired off a fist or foot, "disjunct", several in rapid
  succession (Aang vs the practice dummy; Korra on Amon in "Endgame"). A ring-shaped puff with a bright rim.
- **Air swipe / air blade / gale slice** (ours: Slice). A crescent of compressed air from a sweeping arm or staff,
  "capable of deflecting colossal projectiles" or cutting stone and timber (Aang vs Azula in "The Day of Black Sun,
  Part 2"). A thin white crescent with a sharp outer rim that grows as it flies; the staff makes it narrower and
  cleaner (memory). Gather: a wide sweep; release: the crescent leaves on the follow-through.
- **Air ball / air scooter** (ours: no direct move; Wind Run is nearest). The ball is made by circling the hands; the
  scooter is "a spherical 'ball' of air ... balancing on it like a top" that carries the rider quickly and over
  vertical surfaces (Aang in "The Avatar Returns", "The Drill"). A pale sphere with spiral surface lines that spins;
  spin-up 6-12 frames; it deflates into a puff of dust when dropped. Aang invented it as a child ("The Storm" shows the
  game he played with it).
- **Air sphere** (Avatar-level; ours: Wind Guard is the nearest). A sphere round the body that allows flight and
  shields against rock (Aang in the Avatar State, "Sozin's Comet, Part 4"). Translucent shell with swirling surface
  lines and a bright rim; it appears over 12-20 frames and pops outward when dropped.
- **Air shield / cocoon / cushion** (ours: Wind Guard). "Throwing up gusts of air close to their bodies as a shield ...
  rarely to stop attacks directly and more often to push the attack aside"; the cocoon is a spin that wraps the body
  in wind; the cushion softens a fall. Concentric rings and a ring of spiralling lines round the body (memory).
- **Air vortex / funnel / tornado / spout** (ours: Wind Wall, as a column). A spinning funnel that traps or deflects
  (Aang vs a boulder in "The King of Omashu"); the mini-tornado used to climb or dodge lightning ("Sozin's Comet,
  Part 3"); the spout that lifts the bender; many airbenders together make a big tornado (Jinora's group in "Venom of
  the Red Lotus"). A column of stacked pale arcs and spiral lines with debris orbiting.
- **Wind run / smooth stride / enhanced speed** (ours: Wind Run). The bender decreases air resistance and runs fast, up
  walls, even briefly across water; "a speed almost too swift for the naked eye" (Aang). Drawn as a body smear with
  streak lines trailing and a small dust line at the feet.
- **Evasion** (ours: Slip, Air Redirect). Reed in the wind, twisting wind, sense the blow's wake: the bender flows
  round blows; returning wind pulls thrown objects into a cyclone and sends them back; stunning stance returns a
  blast after being struck. Ghost-like body doubles on the dodge frames (memory).
- **Leap / levitation** (ours: Leap). "Jump high and far by riding on strong gusts of wind"; gliding takeoff;
  shockwave on landing.
- **Flight** (rare). Guru Laghima, then Zaheer (Korra, "Enter the Void"): effortless flight without a glider.

| Slice | air swipe, air blade | wide sweep, thin crescent with a sharp rim leaves on the follow-through |
| Slip | reed in the wind, twisting wind | body flows round the blow, ghost doubles on the dodge frames |
| Wind Run | wind run, air scooter | body smear, streak lines, dust line at the feet |
| Wind Guard | air shield, cocoon, sphere | rings and spiral lines round the body, a sphere shell on the big version |
| Wind Wall | air vortex, funnel | a column of stacked pale arcs with debris orbiting |
| Air Redirect | returning wind, air swipe deflection | the incoming rock or bolt curves into a cyclone and goes back |
| Leap | wind-assisted jump, shockwave landing | gust under the feet, shockwave ring on landing |

Air has no source to gather, so the cast is the sweep: draw the circle (arms and waist), let the pale arcs follow, draw
them in, then release. Pick up dust and light debris from the deck so the viewer sees the air by what it moves.
