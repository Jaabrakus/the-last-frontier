# Fire: how it is produced and shaped (reference)

**Sources and honesty.** Technique names, users and episodes come from the Avatar Wiki's Firebending page and its

## 1. The art behind it

- Fire is Northern Shaolin (changquan, "long fist"): "kicking over hand fighting", long aggressive techniques that
  "explode and drive through the opponent", with some Northern Praying Mantis and xingyi (the "seven stars": hands,
  feet, elbows, hips, knees, shoulders, head). Quick successive attacks with bursts of extreme power ("ging lik")
  and a pre-emptive strike. Fire is the most aggressive art and attacks first. (Wiki: Connection, Opposing art.)
- Fire comes from the body: "Firebenders use their chi as a source for their bending ... a sharp contrast to the other
  bending arts, which manipulate already present sources", though they can also control or enhance nearby flames.
  Breath control is the foundation: Iroh says true power "comes not from physical size or strength, but from the very
  control of one's breath"; beginners do breathing exercises first (Jeong Jeong in "The Deserter"). (Wiki:
  Weaknesses: poor breath control; Fighting style.)
- Fire's weakness is defence and emotion: anger boosts flames but costs control; the strongest bending needs "inner
  fire" (drive). Circular motion "powers up" the flame; lightning is made with slow circles. (Wiki: Fighting style.)
- Close and distance fire use different styles; the Dancing Dragon form (Sun Warriors) is fluid and circular
  ("The Firebending Masters").

## 2. How fire is produced (not gathered)

- **From breath and the strike.** Inhale and brace (chest and shoulders rise), the weight sinks into the hips, then
  the limb snaps out and the fire leaves with the breath. The flame buds at the knuckle or toe on the extension
  frame, not before (memory). The exhale and the strike are the same beat.
- **From existing flames.** Candles, torches, campfires: the bender draws the flame up and bigger ("fan the flame",
  wiki: Zuko's candles; Jeong Jeong; the throne-room fires rising with Ozai's anger; Azula's torch burning blue).
  Fire manipulation: "control the size and intensity of any nearby flames and can draw them in" (Roku, Zuko).
- **Charging.** A held stance with the fire growing in the hand or at the foot: "Charged attacks: ... charging their
  attacks before releasing them, allowing them to create enormous blasts" (Azula on the drill, Iroh at the Inner
  Wall, Ozai). The flame grows and darkens at the core, the bender's body trembles (memory).
- **Lightning is the exception: it is drawn.** "Generating lightning ... by separating the yin and yang energies":
  slow circular arm motions, then two fingers lead the bolt down a straight line. It needs calm; anger makes only a
  small explosion (wiki: Zuko in "Bitter Work"). Typical: 1-2 s of circling, hold 4-6 frames, the bolt 2-3 frames.
- **Heat without flame:** smoke, steam, concussive pulses (wiki: heat control). Used sparingly in the show.
- **Timing of the production** (typical): jab or punch wind-up 3-4 frames, extension 2 frames, recoil 4 frames; kick
  chambers 4-6 frames, the swing 2-3 frames; charged blasts 12-24 frames of growth; breath: inhale 8-12 frames, a
  held exhale 10-20 frames.

## 3. The shapes of the classic moves

"Ours" is the game's move the shape maps to (section 7).

- **Fire punch / jab / fist** (ours: Punch, Embers). "Jabs and punches produce miniature fireballs and missiles of
  flame ... charged up to create larger, slower bursts, or swiftly and repetitively fired to keep opponents off
  balance." A teardrop of three bands (red-orange rim, orange, pale core) with a tail of detached tongues, leaving
  the fist on the extension frame, stretched (smear) for 1-2 frames, then spinning slightly in flight (memory). It
  flies 8-14 frames and burns out into a smoke puff and a few embers, or bursts on a hit with a flash and a radial
  puff. Embers: a rapid string of small ones with multiples of the fist.
- **Fire kick / arc / ring** (ours: Kick). "Spinning kicks or sweeping arm movements create rings and arcs" that
  slice wide targets; a spin kick throws an expanding ring (Zuko vs Azula in the comet Agni Kai); a somersault kick
  throws an arc upward; a dropkick throws fire off the foot (Azula on the drill; Korra vs Zaheer). The arc is a
  crescent of flame with a thick leading edge and a thin tail, widening as it flies (memory).
- **Fire stream, whip, lash** (ours: no direct move; Punch held is nearest). A stream from the palm or fingertips,
  widened into a flamethrower; the whip "has a semi-tactile quality" (Zuko vs Katara's water whips in "The Crossroads
  of Destiny"; the Fire Nation circus trainer in "Appa's Lost Days"); lashes brought down from above (Azula in "The
  Chase"). A long ribbon of flame with a whipped, curling tip and trailing sparks (memory).
- **Breath of fire / fire breathing** (ours: Breath). "Mixing air from the lungs with the flame, creating a wider,
  hotter blast"; spontaneous in rage or trained for years (Iroh, "the Dragon of the West"; Zuko). A wide cone from the
  mouth, brightest at the base, edge frayed; the wall of fiery breath keeps foes back. Smoke curls from the mouth
  afterwards (memory). Breath of fire also warms the body ("The Boiling Rock").
- **Wall / shield of flames** (ours: Fire Wall). Jeong Jeong's wall that burns on water to stop patrol boats ("The
  Deserter"); Azula's wall vs Katara; the flame shield that deflects attacks "from all four elements" (Azula, "The
  Chase"). A curtain of tall tongues swaying from a single base, with a bright edge and dark smoke on top.
- **Jet propulsion, jet-stepping, fire dash** (ours: Jets, Step). Flames from the hands or feet throw the bender
  forwards or up: "released the flames she used to propel herself horizontally" (Azula, first in "The Crossroads of
  Destiny"; over the lake in "The Boiling Rock, Part 2"). Short bursts of jets under the feet let the bender run on
  air. Two blue-white plumes behind the body, a smear on the body itself (memory).
- **Lightning** (ours: Lightning). Generation as above; the bolt is a jagged white core with a blue halo and
  branching forks, flickering 1-2 frames per shape; on a hit a flash, a spray of sparks and the target thrown back.
  Redirection: absorb through one arm, guide through the stomach, send out the other (Iroh in "Bitter Work";
  Zuko in "Sozin's Comet, Part 3"). Azula's bolt is blue.
- **Parry / blocks** (ours: Parry). "Defuse and extinguish an oncoming fire blast ... by using a swift kick, jab, or
  any other defensive maneuver"; flame redirection "using fluid motion" (wiki: Blocking fire; Flame redirection). A
  sweeping kick or palm that knocks the blast sideways into a spray of sparks.
- **Blue fire.** Hotter than orange; Azula's signature (wiki: Special techniques); same shapes, cooler palette.
- **Rarer:** ring of flames, fire pinwheel (Azula in "Return to Omashu"), fire bomb ("The Ember Island Players"),
  white fire, dragon fire; see the wiki page.

## 4. Timing at a glance (typical 2D figures)

| Move | Production | Release | Travel | End |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Punch | wind-up 3-4 frames | extension 2 frames, bolt stretched | 8-14 frames | burns out in a puff or bursts |
| Embers | 2-3 frames each | multiples of the fist | 6-10 frames | tiny puffs |
| Kick arc | chamber 4-6 | swing 2-3 | arc widens, 8-14 frames | tongues detach, puff |
| Breath | inhale 8-12 frames | exhale 10-20 frames held | cone, 10-20 frames | smoke curl |
| Fire wall | stomp or sweep 6-8 | rises 4-6 | stationary | sinks, smoke |
| Lightning | circle 1-2 s, hold 4-6 frames | bolt 2-3 frames | instant | flash, sparks |
| Jets | crouch 4-6 | burst 2-4 frames | body smeared | plumes shrink to puffs |

## 5. Reference moments (episode pages are `https://avatar.fandom.com/wiki/<Title_With_Underscores>`)

1. **"The Southern Air Temple"** (1.3). Zuko vs Zhao's Agni Kai at Ketu Harbor: the shed shawl, fire punches and
   kicks traded along straight lines, one fire blast knocked aside by a kick. Look at: bolts leaving the fist on
   the extension frame, and the pause before each exchange.
2. **"The Storm"** (1.12). The flashback Agni Kai between Zuko and Ozai. Look at: how a single heavy fireball is
   built up and driven at the camera.
3. **"The Deserter"** (1.16). Jeong Jeong: breathing exercises, candle flames fanned, the wall of flames burning on
   water, Aang burning Katara by accident. Look at: fire drawn as something alive; the wall's tall tongues on water.
4. **"Winter Solstice, Part 2: Avatar Roku"** (1.8). Roku's controlled blast melts the chains without a burn. Look
   at: a huge pale flash with no flames touching the people.
5. **"The Chase"** (2.8). Azula's lash and flame shield against several elements. Look at: the lash's curling tip;
   the shield as a quick disc of flame.
6. **"The Drill"** (2.13). Azula's fire dropkick and charged blasts against Aang. Look at: flame off the foot;
   a charged blast growing before release.
7. **"The Crossroads of Destiny"** (2.20). Azula's blue fire; first jet propulsion; lightning; Zuko's fire whips
   against Katara's water whips. Look at: the blue palette; the lightning wind-up of slow circles then a straight
   snap; fire meeting water as steam.
8. **"The Firebending Masters"** (3.13). Ran and Shaw's dance with rainbow dragon fire and the ring of flames; the
   Dancing Dragon form. Look at: fire as a flowing ribbon in a circle; the opposite of Azula's.
9. **"Sozin's Comet, Part 3: Into the Inferno"** (3.20). Zuko vs Azula's comet Agni Kai: the expanding ring from a
   spin kick, jet propulsion, Azula's lightning and Zuko's redirect; Iroh's charged blast at the Inner Wall. Look
   at: the ring, the jagged lightning, the redirect path through the body.
10. **"Sozin's Comet, Part 4: Avatar Aang"** (3.21). Ozai's comet fire vs Aang; Aang's fire whips. Look at: fire at
    screen-filling scale; five whips at once.
11. **Korra: "A Leaf in the Wind"** (Book One, 2), **"And the Winner Is..."** (1.6) and **"Endgame"** (1.12). The
    Fire Ferrets' pro-bending matches (Mako's quick fire jabs, per the wiki's note on fire in the ring); Korra's
    finger flame jet cutting chains in "Endgame" (wiki). Look at: quick boxed releases and recoveries.
12. **Korra: "Venom of the Red Lotus"** (Book Three, 13). Korra's fire dropkick and jet propulsion against Zaheer
    (wiki). Look at: Studio Mir's smears on fast fire kicks.

## 7. Mapping to our fire moves (`data/sway/moves.json` labels)

| Ours | Show shape to study | Notes for the production |
|---|---|---|
| Punch | fire punch / fist, charged blast | bud at the knuckle on the extension frame, teardrop with tongues, smear, burn out in a puff |
| Embers | fire jabs | rapid small bolts with fist multiples |
| Kick | fire kick, ring, arc, dropkick | crescent that widens; ring from a spin kick; the ground wave reads as a low ring of flame |
| Breath | breath of fire | inhale beat, then a fraying cone held; smoke curl after |
| Fire Wall | wall of flames | stomp or sweep, tall swaying tongues, dark smoke on top |
| Jets, Step | jet propulsion, jet-stepping | plumes behind the body, smear on the fighter |
| Lightning | lightning generation and redirection | slow circles, two-finger lead, straight snap; jagged white core, blue halo |
| Parry | blocking fire, redirection | swept kick or palm knocks the bolt aside into sparks |

The ring has no fuel to gather, so the cast is the body: breath in, weight down, strike, with the fire appearing at the
limb on the snap. Where the deck has flames already (a wall, an earlier bolt) a fighter may pull them in as in "fan
the flame".
