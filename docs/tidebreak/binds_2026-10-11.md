# Binds — 11 October 2026

The owner, after the role wheel: "i think each element should have binds and finishers and how they build team comps is on
them right?" Every element already had finishers; binds had been cut on 5 October (every root and slow on a player). His
answers to three questions brought them back in this shape:

- **How a bind holds:** the feet only. For about 0.7 s the bound player cannot walk, jump, roll, dodge or dash; aim, casting
  and the guard stay his. Each bind on the same person within 6 s holds for less.
- **Where binds live:** as skill tree twists, one per element, "this or that" like the Lightning Pistol. Classic plays as
  before; a player who wants a bind trades his move's launch for it.
- **Fire's bind:** a lightning shock.

## The four binds

| Element | Twist (`id`) | Of | What it does | Trade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Earth | Stone Grip (`earth.column.grip`) | the Column (2, tap twice) | stone closes round his feet where the Column would rise | no launch; 5% (from 9) |
| Water | Frost Geyser (`water.geyser.frost`) | the Geyser (2, tap twice) | ice closes round his feet where the Geyser would rise | no launch; 4% (from 7) |
| Fire | Static Shock (`fire.lightning.shock`) | Lightning (1, tap twice) | a quicker Lightning (1.4 s charge, from 2.2) whose strike shocks his feet still | 8% (from 25), never a knockout |
| Air | Tripwind (`air.pull.trip`) | the Pull (2, tap twice) | the Pull's wave sweeps his legs where he stands instead of dragging him in | no pull; 4% (from 7) |

Each is a Twist (2 points), so it is one of that move's twists: Static Shock excludes Short Fuse, Long Lightning and the
Lightning Pistol (all change the same charge), and they exclude it.

## The rules (`data/ring/rules.json` `stock.bind`)

- The hold is 0.7 s (`bind_s`, the twist's spec key). Again on the same body within `memory_s` 6 s: x `decay` 0.6 each time
  (0.42 s, then 0.25 s), and nothing once under `floor_s` 0.2 s. Six quiet seconds and it is whole again. A new round clears it.
- On the deck only: a body in the air is not bound (the hit adds its % and nothing more).
- No launch, no hitstun, no string hit: the bind replaces what the move's hit would do.
- A perfect guard stops it, a late guard halves it (as every hit). A hit that launches or pushes him frees his feet at once.
- Allies are immune, as to every bend.

## Where it lives in the code

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| The twist's number | `spec:<move>/<mode>.bind_s` from none to 0.7: a spec key Classic lacks (`BendLoadout.NEW_SPEC_KEYS`), merged for the wearer as every spec change |
| The hit carries it | `BendR3.info_for` copies `bind_s` onto the cast's hit info |
| The hold | `RingRules.on_hit`: a hit with `bind_s` adds its % (the wearer's class: the twist's lower d) and calls `BendStatus.bind` in place of the launch |
| The feet held | `BendStatus.bind` (the decay, the deck-only rule) sets the same root as the open world's (`_timed`: the player's `bend_speed_cap` 0, a bot's `feet_held`); `BendStatus.unbind` from `_stock_hit` on any launch; `BendStatus.bound` asks |
| No jump, hop, drop or roll | `RingMoves._bound()`: `_on_jump_press`, the short hop, Shift + Space's drop, the guard's ground dodges |
| No dash | `BendEffects._apply`: a bound caster's dash-key move (key 3, `DASH_KEY`: Burrow, Skate, Surf, Step, Jets, Slip, Leap) is recorded as `held` and goes nowhere; earth's Lift (`FormEffects.pillar` self) the same |
| The looks | `BendLooks.bind_tell` (Stone Grip and Frost Geyser show cracks or frost spreading, then stone or ice teeth, in place of the Column's pillar and the Geyser's water); `BendLooks.BindLook`, solid for the whole hold in the move's own matter (`Bending.element_of` the hit's move): a cuff of stone chunks up the shins (the game's rock facets), ice shards on a disc of ice (its ice facets), four lightning strips crackling up the legs with sparks thrown off, or two of the Wind Ball's spinning wind bands round the legs (broken up by the wind shader's noise, never a clean ring); it rises in 0.08 s and sinks, melts or fades in 0.16 s at the end, or at once when a launch breaks it (`bind_end`, with a puff). Nothing is drawn on anyone above the legs. Like every effect they thin out within 2.5-4 m of the camera. Stills: `tools/ring/bind_shots.gd`. |

## Tests

`tests/ring/test_binds.gd` (36 checks, a real fire-against-earth ring match): the four twists live, the wearer's spec and
hit info carry the bind and Classic's do not; a real hit holds the bot 0.7 s with no launch and the grip's 5%; 0.42, 0.25,
then none; whole again after 6 s; a late guard halves it, a perfect one stops it; a push frees him; it ends on its own; the
held player cannot jump and his dash goes nowhere, but his Punch flies and his guard goes up; Stone Grip raises no pillar.
`tests/ring/test_loadout_apply.gd` checks every twist's changes on a wearer and on a body that wears nothing.

## Not yet

- **The fairness soak**: the four are live on the owner's word with starting numbers (0.7 s, the lower %), not soaked.
- **Bots** never wear a loadout in a match; a bot that is bound stands and still casts and guards. Bots that follow a mate's
  bind with a finisher are part of the team work the role wheel asked for next.
- **Sounds**: a bind plays its move's own hit sound; no sound of its own yet.
