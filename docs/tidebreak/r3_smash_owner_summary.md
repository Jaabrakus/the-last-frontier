# Tidebreak's % ring: how it plays now (one page)

**It's Smash with your number keys.** There's no health bar. Every hit adds %, and the higher your % the farther you fly.
You're out only when you land in the water: the deck is now raised 6 m over a deep tank, with a curtain of falling
water around it. You get 3 lives, and your % resets after each fall. The game is 8 minutes. If lives are tied at the
bell, it goes to sudden death at 300%.

**The keys, in one sentence:** the numbers ready a move and right click casts it (as in round 2); left click, E, 3 and
Space act at once.
- **Space** jumps. Press it again in the air for the double jump. Press it once more when you're off the deck for your
  element's **Rise** (your up-B), which leaves you helpless until you land or grab the edge.
- **3** is the dash on the deck and the **air dodge** in the air. It never changes the move you have ready.
- **E** is your shield: tap it for the timed guard, or hold it to keep it up. Shield plus a tapped direction **rolls**,
  and shield plus **C** spot-dodges.
- **C** is down: crouch, or fast fall in the air.
- **The edge** is grabbed automatically. Any direction key climbs, Space jumps, E rolls in, a click attacks, and C lets
  go.
- **E just before you land** while tumbling is a tech ("break your fall").
- **Hold left or right while you're thrown** to DI ("steer").

**Each element in the air**

| | Double jump | Rise (up-B) | Comes back from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earth (heaviest, dies last) | 1.4 m, off a crumbling slab | **Pillar**: a stone column from the water, leaning toward the deck | 11 m out |
| Water (the middle) | 1.6 m, a splash | **Fountain**: a quick water column, full drift | 12 m out |
| Fire | 1.6 m, foot jets | **Jet**: a hover you can be punished in, then a 7.5 m burst where you aim | 16 m out |
| Air (lightest, dies first) | 1.9 m, a gust | **Updraft**: a lift, then a slow glide | 17 m out |

A Rise that comes up a little short still scrambles up the stage wall to the edge. A double jump also cancels half the
throw's momentum, so mashing Space saves newcomers more often than doing nothing.

**Air moves:** everything goes where you aim. **2 in the air is the Slam** (your down-air): it spikes off the edge, and
on the deck it bounces him up. Earth's Slam is the Hammer, water's the Downpour (it leaves a puddle), fire's the Axe Kick
(it sends out a small fire wave) and air's the Down Gust (it lifts you). Hop and release at the same moment for a short
hop. Landing mid-move costs you landing lag.

**Combos:** click on him three times and the third hit is the **Pop**, which pops him up. Then:
- earth: the Pop leaves a stone in your hand, so press E to throw it back at him;
- water, fire, air: right-click your 1.

That works at every %. Combos end by themselves (hitstun shrinks with each hit, and the 6th hit always frees him). The
same move used again and again hits softer. Long-range shots build %, but KOs come up close. Earth catching a stone and
throwing it back is still the heart of it: every return flies faster, up to a KO at about 75%.

**I decided the rest:** heal (5) only takes back damage from the last 6 seconds, and never while he's respawning. No
trail on flying bodies and no drip on respawned ones (the images of both are there if you want them). The % plates sit in
the top corners. Bots play with exactly your moves.

**Questions only you can answer**
1. **Hitstun:** when you're hit, you can't act for a moment (0.12-0.4 s at low %, never more than 0.6 s before you can
   break out). Your aim and charge are never touched. Is that OK under your "getting hit never slows the player" rule?
   *(I built: yes.)*
2. **Camera:** if you're thrown off facing away from the deck and you're not moving the mouse, the view turns toward the
   deck. Any mouse move stops it. OK, or should the view never turn? *(I built: it turns.)*
3. **Stale moves** cut the % a repeated move deals, but never stop you casting it. Is that inside "lock time is the only
   spam limit"? *(I built: yes.)*
4. Do you want **one air-only close hit per element**, with its own animation, next round? *(I'd say yes.)*
5. **The new arena look:** water 6 m down, piers, the water curtain, a pale stone edge with lanterns, and the stands 4 m
   back. Noon and night images come first. *(I built it.)*
