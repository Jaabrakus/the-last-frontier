**The Lightning Pistol** is fire's new Storm twist (`fire.lightning.pistol` in `data/sway/skill_tree.json`; live in
`data/bending/loadout_apply.json`). In the Advancement window it sits on Lightning's line as a "Choose one" with Forked
Lightning (one twist per move), and it cannot be worn with Short Fuse or Long Lightning (they set the same charge).

The numbers are starting values for the owner to try; the node has not been through the fairness soak
(`tools/ring/loadout_soak.gd`) yet.

To try it: Advancement window, fire, a loadout slot, Lightning's line, the "Choose one" octagon, Lightning Pistol; wear the

## How the Lightning Pistol is built (the Frontier tech, reused)

- `tools/anim_author/bake_bend_aim.gd` bakes `frontier-pistol-lightning` into `animations_extra/Frontier_Bend_Aim.res` on our
  own Lightning's timeline (length, `hold_s`, `strike_s`, `back_s`): the body is the pistol's standing aim exactly as
  `PlayerAnim` builds it (the twisted stance `actor_b-p-pistol-aim-idle` from `Frontier_Downgraded.res`, `STAND_AIM_ARM`
  `own-pistol-bow-arm` for the right arm, `actor_b-p-relax-idle` at phase 0 for the hanging left arm), our own two-finger hand
  (from `own-bend-fire-lightning-duel`) turned at the wrist to go on along the forearm, raised from fire's stance over 0.3 s, a
  kick at the strike, lowered from `back_s`.
- In the game `SwayPose` shows a pick with `aim: "pistol"` as the pistol's own aims are: standing the whole clip, moving the
  right arm alone over the jog's body; no spine lean. `BendAimArm` (`scripts/bending/bend_aim_arm.gd`, the pistol's
  `JogAimArm` with the two fingers for the muzzle; `JogAimArm._pointer` is the one seam added to it) sits right after the cast
  pose, turns the arm onto the crosshair and the head toward it, and puts the kick back after its turn.
- A worn twist's pose is a new kind of node change, `pose:<move>/<mode>` (`BendLoadout`'s `pose` overlay, `pose_of`), read by
  `BendPoses.pick` for the wearer before his fighting style. Bots never wear one (they cast the original).
- Provenance: the clip's body and arms come from the base library (`animations/Actor_B.res`, lineage not yet verified), so it
  is named `frontier-` (not `own-`), lives in its own library, and `BendPoses` plays it only because `poses.json`
  `frontier_tech` lists it. `animations/SOURCE.md` has its row and inputs.

## A timing fix found on the way (this morning's moving hold)

The Advancement review test (`tests/ring/test_loadout_review.gd`) compares Classic against a saved fingerprint. It showed
that the moving hold shipped this morning (`ed7a41d9`) let some strikes land one frame off (late for most clips whose strike
comes within 0.05 s of the hold: the air Slice, the punches, the Boxing flurry; a frame either way where float rounding fell
on an exact frame). Fixed in `SwayPose`: the release's catch-up is made up a little before the strike (`_catch_s`), and once
made up the release runs from exactly the held moment (`_from_held`), so every strike, end and rooting frame is the old one.

Classic's fingerprint (`tests/ring/fixtures/loadout_classic_golden.json`) was re-taken after that, on purpose. Against the old
one: the 124 locks and 47 real casts are identical; of 484 picks only the 8 Embers and Ice Spear entries differ (the clips);
of 389 cast timelines no strike, end, root or wait frame differs; 267 differ in their visual trace only (the moving hold's
creep, the new clips), and the Ice Spear's other-hand entry is gone (the rifle has no other hand).
