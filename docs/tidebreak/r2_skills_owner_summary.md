# Tidebreak skills, round 2: the one-page version

You asked for: "bring back the kick make it easy to use and correct to adjust ... easy hard to master ... think human made
think avg IQ". This is the plan. Nothing is built yet. The questions at the bottom are the calls only you can make.

## What changes for the player

- **Same buttons for every element, one job each.**
  - Left click: **Jab**.
  - **E: Guard.** It has its own button now, so you never have to switch to it first.
  - 1: **Strike**, your shot.
  - 2: **Push**, your body move. Hold it longer and it grows: you hear a second click.
  - 3: **Dash**.
  - 4: **Wall**.
  - 5: **Heal**.
  - Space jumps, even while you hold a cast.
- **No memory when picking.** Tap a key and you get its first move; tap it twice and you get the second. 1 is always
  Spike, so you never have to wonder what you left readied. The first match shows only the four first moves.
- **A click always does something.** Click and the move goes as soon as it's ready. Hold, and you aim while it fills.
  Let go early and it's called off for free, which is how you fake. While you hold, the body turns with your aim.
- **Guarding is one sentence:** "E: on the beat it's yours. Late, half gets through. Earth always catches stone."
  - Water swings the shot round you and sends it back.
  - Fire sends it straight back, and is the only one that can send lightning back.
  - Air bounces it back and gets a stronger next hit.
  - A guard that catches nothing makes your next guard slower, so mashing E doesn't pay.
- **Earth's catch is never weaker than today.** A late catch is still a full catch, with no slide. On top of that:
  - Catch just before it hits and your throw back is faster and harder.
  - The stone gets faster with every return, so earth vs earth becomes a rally.
  - It takes two taps: E to catch, E again to throw.
- **Getting hit no longer wipes your cast.** It knocks it back a bit, and your dash and guard always go. Lightning's
  charge still breaks on any hit.
- **One passive each:**
  - Earth, **Rooted**: stand still and you lock faster and get pushed less.
  - Water, **Flowing**: your water bends a little after it leaves.
  - Fire, **Footwork**: full speed locks on the run.
  - Air, **Carried wind**: hit straight out of a dash and you knock harder.

## The fire Kick

- The **Kick** is back exactly as you described it: **a wave of fire running along the ground** from the foot.
  - Aim at him and it runs at him.
  - He jumps it, or a wall stops it.
  - Hold it longer and the wave comes wider and longer.
- The flame wheel from wave 3 is gone.
- The low knee is the tell: we key a new low sweeping kick by hand, and the Kick doesn't ship until that clip exists, so
  the pose never lies.
- Optional, your call: a **High Kick** on the same key (tap twice). It is a flame crescent over walls and into jumpers,
  using the old waist-high kick.

## What got cut

- **Every root and slow that can land on a player:** trap, sink, freeze, ice patch, vacuum, trip, flare.
- **The "skill with three variations" layer.** Each element goes from 24 moves to 7, plus Jab, Guard and Patch.
- **Fire:** the flame wheel, Breath, sweep, ring, inferno, stream, double jab, Embers and the fireball. Punch stays a
  plain punch, and Lightning is now Punch's partner on 1.
- **Earth:** the disc, stomp, shell, ramp, the free pillar, Anchor, and the other skates.
- **Water:** the Frost skill, tide, bubble, spray, shards, draw, the Lash jet and the water meter's spending.
- **Air:** the tornado, wind run, whirl ride, Cyclone, and the 2.5 s auto-return shield.
- **Hidden rules:** "any attack throws what you hold", picking the kick by where the crosshair points, Simple's
  near/far switch, and Lift's footstep timing.

## Questions only you can answer

1. **Layout.** Is the button layout above OK? It moves earth's wall to 4 and Shove to 2, and Lightning to 1.
2. **The Kick.** Always the ground wave, as above? Cut the flame wheel?
3. **High Kick.** Add it as the Kick's partner? Or make "a Kick done in the air" throw the crescent? Or have no crescent
   at all?
4. **Fire cuts.** Is it OK to lose the fireball, Embers and Breath?
5. **Guards.** Perfect = yours, late = half, earth always catches stone, a missed guard makes the next one slower. OK?
6. **Earth's rally.** Faster returns are in. Should a caught stone drop after 5 s (today it never does)? Should long
   rallies end by sliding a late catcher back a little? Both are "no" for now, to keep your catch as it is.
7. **Roots and slows.** Cut all of them on players, including earth's Anchor?
8. **Hits.** Should a hit knock a cast back instead of wiping it?
9. **Water meter.** Retire it (lock time is the only limit), or keep 6 visible drops?
10. **Air.** Should the Gust become a travelling wind wave you can dodge? Should a new **Wind Wall** go on 4, or should
    Cyclone stay there?
11. **Water.** Should Turn replace Redirect, and Geyser join Wave on 2? The Wave stays today's cone.
12. **Earth.** Should the Spike rise and hang while you aim, and the disc be cut?
13. **Stone vs stone.** Should the bigger stone win, instead of both breaking?
14. **Column and Geyser.** They start small and grow for 0.35 s, with no mark on the floor. Is that a fair tell, or too
    close to a target cue?
15. **Passives.** Are the four passives above OK?
16. **Names.** Water Whip → **Lash**, Redirect → **Turn**, Air Ball → **Wind Ball**, Air Shield → **Wind Guard**,
    Spout → **Geyser**. Do you want other names?
17. **Schemes.** Keys stays the default; the others move under "Other ways". "First moves only" replaces Simple. Should
    Direction stay on PC?
18. **Walls.** Two each (fire and wind walls one), placed where you aim. Should walls also last 30 s instead of 60?
19. **Jabs.** Should every jab be close range, cutting Pebbles' reach from 30 m to 10 m?
20. **Lightning.** Should it flash on the caster's hand for a quarter second before it strikes, so it can be dodged?
21. **Water extras.** Do you want either of the water designer's ideas, "rise" (a geyser under yourself) or "undertow"?
22. **Outside the kit.** Does "no cel shading" also cover the cel-banded effects? Should the player's slower first casts
    be flattened in matches?
