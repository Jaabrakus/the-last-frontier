# Owner answers for round 3 (2026-10-05, after round 1's review)

## The SMASH LAYER (the owner, later the same day; supersedes the "closing tide" answer below)

"instead of a knock back we should implement a smash bros 100% plus like arena out system we should give everyone all
movement options and in air options and combos"

1. **Damage % instead of health and zone knock-backs.** No health bar: every hit adds damage %, and the higher a
   competitor's %, the further (and the longer the hitstun of) the next launch. A competitor is out only when he lands
   in the WATER. The pro-bending zone lines (fall back a zone) are retired in this mode; the deck is the stage.
2. **3 STOCKS** each; % resets to 0 after each KO; last one standing wins (Smash's rule). A respawn that is fair and
   readable (the KO'd competitor comes back above his end of the deck after a short pause, briefly safe).
3. **Full RECOVERY:** knocked off the deck you can fight your way back — double jump, air dodge, an up-recovery move per
   element, and grabbing the deck's EDGE (hang, then climb / jump / roll / attack up). Out only in the water.
4. **Every element gets ALL the movement options:** run, short hop / full hop, double jump, fast fall, directional air
   dodge, spot dodge, rolls, shield (the E Guard), edge grab and the edge options; and IN-AIR options for the moves
   (aerial versions) and COMBOS (hitstun that grows with % makes strings; jab strings; cancels).
5. **Controls: KEEP THE NUMBER KEYS** (the owner chose this over Smash's two-button layout): round 2's layout stays —
   LMB Jab, E Guard, 1 Strike, 2 Push, 3 Dash, 4 Wall, 5 Heal, tap/double-tap. Movement uses Space (jump/double
   jump), the movement keys, and the existing dodge input; aerial versions come from casting while airborne.
6. The "closing tide" answer is superseded by the % system (knocks grow with %). A stalemate breaker only if matches
   still run long (sudden death at the timer, as Smash: everyone at high %, next KO wins).
