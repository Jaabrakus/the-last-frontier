**The float** (`rules.json` `stock.float`, `BendStatus.float_up`): a class with `float_s` (0.8) holds whom it launches near the
top of his launch, sinking only 0.5 m/s (`RingMoves._air_physics`), helpless: his hitstun runs through the rise and the float and
cannot be escaped (`BendKnock.can_escape`), so no steering, no jump or air dodge, no cast, no guard. Each float within 6 s of the
last on him holds 0.6 of the last (0.8, 0.48, 0.29, then none). Any launch ends a float (a follow-up's own launch takes over).

**The Column never knocked anyone up** (the owner, the same evening: "i dont think the knock column knocks someone up right?").
Its pillar (3 m, `pillar.height_m`) rises under the man it is cast at in 0.35 s and carries him onto its top; its throw at the
peak (`BendEffects.status_at`) looked for bodies within 3 m of the deck (`BendStatus.near`'s window), and he stood exactly 3 m
up: no hit, no %, no launch; he just rode the pillar. Older than the knock-ups, hidden because the first knock-up test fed the
Column's hit in directly. Now the throw looks from the pillar's middle (`lift_m`, the pillar's height: the man on its top and
those on the deck beside it are both in reach): as cast, +8.5% at 0%, thrown off the top to 5-5.5 m over the deck, floating
until ~1.65 s, then down onto the pillar or the deck. The Geyser has no solid column (he stays on the deck) and always hit.

**Tapped and held**, in play: the Kick and the Fissure knock up when let go as soon as they are full; held 0.45 s past full
(`grow_after_s`) they grow into the long 40-degree throw (no float). The owner, told: "ah okay thats smart".

## Protecting a held teammate

- **Freeing him:** a teammate's bend that reaches someone held in a bind or a float frees him (`BendStatus.rescue`): a bolt
  passing through him (`BendBolt._rescue_on`), a cone (`BendEffects.cone`), a Column or Geyser at him (`status_at`). A bind
  breaks; a float ends with his hitstun, and he falls free. The float lasts the rise (~0.5 s) and 0.8 s more, so a mate has about
  a second to see it and fire; a bind's 0.7 s is tighter.
- **Walls in front of him:** walls never free him; an Earth Wall or an Ice Wall raised within 3 m of a floating teammate rises
  tall enough to cover him (`BendStatus.float_cover`: his feet's height and 2 m more, from the wall's 2 m), so the follow-up
  hits the wall.

| Twist (`id`) | Of | What it does | Trade |

| Wind Prison (`air.wall.prison`) | Wind Wall (4) | the wall stands 4.5 m (from 2.5: a knock-up floats its man's feet some 3 m up, over the plain wall); whoever it touches **in the air** (a jump, a knock-up) is caught and held inside it for 1 s, helpless, where he is; on the ground it pushes as before | it turns no shot aside |
| Drenching Wave (`water.wave.drench`) | Wave (2) | its hit soaks him: he runs (and drifts in the air) at 0.7 of his speed for 1.5 s; aim, casts, guard and dashes as ever | it pushes softer (base 6.5 from 7.5) |

Wind Prison shares the float's memory (a prison right after a knock-up float holds 0.6 s, and so on). The soak has its own
(`stock.drench`: 1.5, 0.9, 0.54, 0.32 s, then none). Both are spec keys Classic lacks (`prison_s`, `drench_s`:
`BendLoadout.NEW_SPEC_KEYS`), live on the owner's word, not soaked. Deep Fissure now changes the held Fissure only (14.5 m/s, its
throw at 55 degrees): the tapped one is a knock-up for everyone.
