## The one finding for the owner: the Air Lance against water

Matchups that moved since 7 October (wins out of 24): water against air 10 → **2**, earth against air 17 → 10; the rest within
a few games. Air against water only, 24 games a run, same seed:

| Air's key 2 held long | Water wins | Air wins |
|---|---|---|
| the old grown Gust (a 3 m wave) | 11 | 13 |
| the Air Lance as built (40 m/s, 34 m, 12%) | 2 | 22 |
| the Lance slowed to 32 m/s, 28 m, 10% (tried, reverted) | 3 | 21 |

So it is not the Lance's numbers: water's bots have no answer to a fast spear of air (water's Turn takes water and ice, not
air), and air turns those hits into Wind Ball and Gust falls (air's falls on water: Ball 31, Gust 21, Slice 11, Lance 6).
The Lance keeps the spear the owner approved (its speed, reach and damage; only its launch class softened, pass 1). Options, the owner's call:

1. Water's Turn takes the Lance too (a spear of wind is caught and turned like a spear of ice).
2. The Lance only after a longer hold (grow_after_s for the Gust alone), so it comes less often.
3. Water's bots answer it (dash or Ice Wall when an air spear is let go at them): fairer to the bots, changes nothing for
   players.

**The owner chose option 1 (2026-10-11), with the aim free:** "lets have water be able to turn it or catch the lance and
then turn it back as it wraps around the body for a ice lance"; "we can aim it anywhere not just return to sender". Built:
water's Turn on the beat takes the Lance, it turns to ice as it is caught (BendBolt.become), wraps round the turner and
leaves an ice lance along his aim (x1.2, every Turn's return); late, half through; water's bots read the Lance (its wind-up
and its flight, the threat token air_spear) and Turn it. Air against water again, same seed, 24 games:

| Air's key 2 held long | Water wins | Air wins | Water's turned lances |
|---|---|---|---|
| the Air Lance, before the Turn took it | 2 | 22 | — |
| the Air Lance, the Turn takes it | 4 | 20 | 42 hits, 13 falls |

The Turn works and water's turned lances take falls, but air still wins the matchup on its Wind Ball (30 falls) and Gust
(17); the Lance still landed 144 times on water's bots, who meet many late (a held guard: half through) or not at all. Next,
the owner's call: water's bots on the beat more often against it (their read of the Lance's wind-up), air's bots growing the
Gust less often (bend_ai grow 0.35), or the Lance itself.
