# Owner answers, 2026-10-05 (these OVERRIDE spec.md wherever they differ)

Asked after spec v2 (owner_summary.md). The owner's words are quoted; the rest is how they apply.

1. **The wheel's three variations: CUT.** The fixed layout of spec.md stands for every element: LMB Jab · E Guard ·
   1 Strike · 2 Push ▲ · 3 Dash · 4 Wall · 5 Heal; a tap readies a key's first move, a double tap its second, no memory.
   The mouse wheel may step between a key's two moves (spec), nothing more.

2. **Guard on E: YES — "one is good but dont forget the other elements".** One Guard key for all, and every element's
   guard gets the SAME care as earth's catch, not an afterthought:
   - earth Catch (hold, E again throws back; the rally), water Turn (swings the shot round you and sends it back), fire
     Parry (straight back along your aim; the only answer to lightning), air Wind Guard (bounces it back; a stronger next
     hit) — each with its own perfect window and late rule (spec §2.5), its own OWN clip (pose that reads at a glance),
     its own sound (perfect vs late sound different), its own look on the guarder only (no target cues), bots that use
     it well at each preset, a practice-dummy drill and a lesson card, and its own tests (perfect, late, whiff penalty,
     what it can and cannot guard) — a test per element, not just earth.
   - Each element vs each element: the guard matrix (what each guard does to each kind of shot) is complete, written in
     bending_design.md and tested.

3. **Fire's 2 ×2 = HIGH KICK crescent** (spec's recommendation): the Kick is the floor wave (jump it; walls stop it; ▲
   widens it; steered by the aim's direction while held); the High Kick is the crescent over walls and into jumpers. The
   flame wheel is cut.

4. **Fire cuts: "Keep them all"** — the FIREBALL, EMBERS and BREATH stay in fire's kit. (The other fire cuts in the spec —
   sweep, ring, inferno, stream, double jab, flare, the flame wheel — stand.) Placement, keeping one button = one meaning
   as far as possible (KITS decides the numbers; CORE the mechanics; report where each went):
   - **Fireball** = fire's Strike grown: Punch is the tap-and-go fast bolt; HOLD Punch past the second click and it
     becomes the Fireball (bigger, slower, harder push: today's fireball numbers as the start). Fire is the one element
     whose Strike grows (▲ on fire's 1 as well as 2) — say it in the strip and the lesson ("hold to grow it").
     Lightning stays Punch's partner (1 tap twice).
   - **Embers** = fire's Jab (LMB): a short spray of embers from the fist (today's embers: 7 small bolts, spread 9°,
     range capped at the jab's ≤10 m rule unless balance says otherwise). It replaces the 3 m fist-flame jab.
   - **Breath** = fire's 4 tap twice: a short cone of fire breath (today's breath: 4.5 m, 45°) that leaves a SMOKE
     screen where it burned (smoke breaks sight for bots; no slow on anyone). Fire Wall stays 4's first move.

5. **Everything else** in owner_summary.md's question list: the spec's recommendations stand (they follow rules the owner
   already set — no roots/slows on players, a hit knocks a cast back instead of wiping it, the water meter's spending
   retired, the lightning hand flash, Column/Geyser grow with no floor mark, bigger stone wins, the names, Keys default
   with the other schemes under "Other ways", earth's caught stone never drops and no slide on late catches).
