# Barlow fonts

Barlow Condensed (ExtraBold, Bold, SemiBold) and Barlow Semi Condensed (Medium, SemiBold), plus the licence text.

- Source: Google Fonts' open repository, https://github.com/google/fonts (ofl/barlowcondensed, ofl/barlowsemicondensed), main branch, fetched 2026-10-10 with the owner's OK ("work on it", after being told the files and source).
- Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt here). Free to use, bundle and ship in a game; the fonts may not be sold on their own.
- Designer: The Barlow Project Authors (https://github.com/jpt/barlow). Covers Latin including Vietnamese diacritics.
- Used for: the broadcast-style menus (headlines and numerals in Condensed, body and labels in Semi Condensed).
