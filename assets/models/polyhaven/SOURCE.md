# Photoscanned models for item icons (Poly Haven)

Used only to render inventory and HUD icons (`scripts/ui/inventory/item_icons.gd`, `ItemIcons.SCANNED`); the
items in the world keep the game's own models. Requested by the user on 2026-09-27 ("there are some free good
looking realistic ones for items") and approved for download ("sounds perfect").

- Source: Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com
- License: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), https://polyhaven.com/license; no attribution required, credited here
  anyway. Each asset's authors are listed on its page.
- Files: each asset's 1k glTF (the .gltf, its .bin and its 1k textures), kept in a folder named after the asset.
