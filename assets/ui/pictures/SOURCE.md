# The game's pictures (main menu, loading screens)

Stills of the game itself (user 2026-09-28: "have our game pictures instead of the paintings"; "use this video for the
stills"), taken from the fishing film `~/Downloads/Frontier_fish_final_2026-09-28.mp4` (recorded in the game, 1280x720,
30 fps), frames with no player in them, saved as JPEG (quality 92). Their titles and captions are in
`scripts/ui/menu/menu_paintings.gd` (`PICTURES`).

| File | Film time | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| shallows.jpg | 5.5 s | the river's shallows by the grass bank, fish in the water |
| deep_water.jpg | 12.5 s | the deeper water by the bank, fish in it |
| river_from_above.jpg | 17.5 s | the river between its grass banks, from above |
| white_water.jpg | 30.0 s | the river's bend and its first rapid under the clouds |

Everything in them is the project's own or credited in `CREDITS.md` (textures: Poly Haven, CC0).

`tools/menu/game_pictures.gd` stages and takes pictures of the game's scenes with no UI, for more stills when wanted.
The period's paintings that were shown here before are in the menu's Museum (`assets/ui/paintings/world/SOURCE.md`).
