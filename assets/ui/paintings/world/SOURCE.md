# Public-domain paintings (the menu and the loading screens)

Real paintings of the period and the place, shown with the game's own painted views (MenuPaintings). Downloaded
2026-09-27 with the owner's approval ("sounds perfect a mix oh our own game and the paintings from the world") from
Wikimedia Commons, as Commons' own 1920-pixel-wide copies of the files named below, unmodified. Each file page states
the licence: public domain (the artist died more than 100 years ago; published before 1931 in the US) or CC0 (the
museum's own dedication). No attribution is required; each is credited in CREDITS.md and on screen (title, artist,
year).
